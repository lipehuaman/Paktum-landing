About us

Meet the Paktum team.

Experience from sports and business, deep technical know-how and a real feeling for people – we are more than software.

Patrick Laux
Founder & CEO

Patrick has contributed to various consulting projects in sports and agency environments and holds a degree in engineering. At Paktum he combines this experience with the insights from his board work in finance, digitalization and administration in local clubs and federations.

Dr. Jens Kaeske
Founder & IT Development

As founder, engineer and board member, Jens connects the worlds of technology and business at Paktum. After his PhD at CERN, he co-founded a software company in the Swiss real estate industry and now drives digital innovation in our team.

Robin Laux
Sales & Partner Care

Robin is the point of contact for our users and those who want to become one. With his background in customer service and sales, he identifies needs early on and helps turn feedback into action.

Jonas Walch
Onboarding & Partner Care

Jonas is our point of contact for onboarding, data integration and the personal support of our users in everyday life. With several years of experience in the service sector, he stands for clear communication and an open ear so new users quickly find their way and no questions go unanswered.

Lipe Huamán
Marketing & Design

Lipe is responsible for Paktum's visual marketing and brand experience. With 6 years of experience across graphic design, web design and branding, he shapes how Paktum looks and feels across every touchpoint, from the landing page to campaigns and product communication.

Rollo die Rolle
AI Assistant

Rollo is your intelligent companion in Paktum. He creates summaries, generates lead lists and answers your questions around the tool.
