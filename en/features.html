Module 01

Every space,
one product.

Rights & Inventory Management

Every marketable right lives in one central product catalog – from the single-sided board to the naming-rights space. Instead of spreadsheets and scattered plans, you see at a glance what's free, reserved or sold. New spaces are created right in the editor: stadium plan, jersey or hospitality area – drawn, named and ready to sell instantly.

Step 1Draw a new advertising space directly onto the stadium plan.

Step 2Set the quota, unit and price – as a season or call-off package.

Step 3Utilization per product runs in real time from that moment on.

  • Season and call-off quotas with quantity, unit and price
  • Visual position editors for stadium, jersey and hospitality
  • Playlists for LED boards straight from inventory

Partner CRM

Partners live in the built-in CRM – or are pulled in via an interface from your existing system. Contact persons, communication and documents are linked directly to the partner account. So everyone on the team sees the full history instantly, with no need to dig through a colleague's inbox.

Step 1A new partner – added natively or synced from your CRM.

Step 2Contact persons and email history hang right off the account.

Step 3Booked rights and contract value at a glance.

  • Native CRM or a connection to your existing system
  • Contact persons with a linked mail account
  • Document archive per partner

Contract & Document Management

A contract bundles league conditions, activation tasks and the booked rights, including stadium positions. Based on your own templates, Paktum generates the finished contract document automatically – barter deals and in-kind benefits included, the things that usually slip through the cracks.

Step 1The starting point is your own template, not a blank page.

Step 2Rights and stadium positions get assigned to the contract.

Step 3Paktum generates the contract document – ready to send.

  • Automatic document generation from your templates
  • League conditions and forecasts per contract
  • Direct link to activation tasks
